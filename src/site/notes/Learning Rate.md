### 1. What does a Learning Rate look like IRL?

Imagine you are blindfolded at the top of a mountain, and your goal is to walk down to the bottom of the valley. You can only feel the ground with your feet to know which way is downhill.

- **The learning rate (αα) is your step size.**
    
- If you take **tiny baby steps** (learning rate too small), it will take you forever to reach the bottom (slow convergence).
    
- If you take **massive leaps** (learning rate too large), you might completely overshoot the bottom of the valley, land on the other side, and keep bouncing back and forth higher and higher up the mountain (divergence).
    
- The perfect learning rate is a normal, steady pace that gets you to the bottom efficiently.
