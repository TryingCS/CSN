#ml 
### What does “regression” mean?

In ML/statistics, **regression** means:

> Predicting a **continuous numeric value** from input features.

Examples:

- Predict house price: 250,000 DA 🏘️
    
- Predict temperature: 31.5°C 🌡️
    
- Predict salary: 85,000 DA 💰️
    

So **انحدار** is actually the standard Arabic translation for “regression” in statistics/ML. It can feel strange because literally it suggests “decline/slope,” but in this context it does **not** mean going backward or getting worse. It comes from the historical term “regression to the mean” (Galton), but in modern ML it simply means modeling a relationship and predicting a number.

Contrast:

- **Regression** → output is a number.
    
- **Classification** → output is a class/label.
    

Important nuance❗️: **Logistic regression** has “regression” in its name, but it is used for **classification**, because it predicts a probability of belonging to a class.
