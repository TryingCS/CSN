# **"slash pages"**
standard pages that live at a root-level URL (like `yoursite.com/now`) and help tell the story of the person behind the site. 
Here are some of the most popular ones you might not have heard of yet:

### ✍️ Personal & Identity
*   **`/about`**: The classic "about me" page — your background, bio, or personal story.
*   **`/carry`**: A list of your everyday carry items (EDC), from your wallet to your keys.
*   **`/defaults`**: The default apps and services you use for specific tasks (email, notes, etc.).
*   **`/verify`**: A page that lists and proves your other online identities, often using cryptographic verification.
*   **`/why`**: A "minifesto" explaining why you have a website and what you hope to do with it.

### 📚 Recommendations & Curation
*   **`/blogroll`**: A list of other blogs and websites you read, follow, or recommend.
*   **`/links` or `/bookmarks`**: A collection of links to sites or resources you want to share or save for later.
*   **`/podroll`**: Exactly like a blogroll, but for podcasts you recommend.
*   **`/postroll`**: A list of specific blog posts you've read that you found particularly interesting or impactful.

### 🛠️ Site & Tech
*   **`/colophon`**: A behind-the-scenes look at how your site is made — the tools, technologies, and hosting you use.
*   **`/changelog` or `/log`**: A summary of the changes and updates you've made to your website over time.
*   **`/self-hosting`**: A page listing the services and software you host and manage yourself.
*   **`/slashes`**: A meta-page that lists all the other slash pages available on your site.

### 🎯 Interests & Quirks
*   **`/interests`**: A place to list and expound on the things you're truly passionate about.
*   **`/top4` or `/topfour`**: Share your definitive ranked list of your top 3 favorites (and an honorable mention) in any topic, like movies, albums, or snacks.
*   **`/hills`**: A list of the "stupid hills you're willing to die on" — your strongly held, perhaps quirky, opinions.
*   **`/til`**: Stands for "Today I Learned" — a collection of concise write-ups on small things you've learned day-to-day.
*   **`/wish`**: A public wishlist that gives people opportunities to connect with you by seeing your passions and goals.

### 🗂️ Directories to Explore
You can also browse directories that aggregate these pages from people across the web:
*   **[slashpages.net](https://slashpages.net)**: The most comprehensive guide and list of slash pages.
*   **[nownownow.com](https://nownownow.com)**: A directory of `/now` pages from around the world.
*   **[uses.tech](https://uses.tech)**: A massive collection of developer `/uses` pages detailing their setups.
*   **[defaults.rknight.me](https://defaults.rknight.me)**: A directory for `/defaults` pages.
*   **[bukmark.club](https://bukmark.club)**: A directory of `/links` and bookmark pages.
