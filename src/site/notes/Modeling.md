#ms 

**Translation:** In Arabic, "Modeling" is **النمذجة** , and "Model" is **نموذج**. "Simulation" is **المحاكاة**.
**So what does "Modeling" really mean here?**  

_"taking what we need from reality to predict something to make better decisions"_ — is **exactly** what the course means. The slides formalize this in two steps:

1. **Modeling (Modélisation):** Creating an _abstract, simplified representation_ of a real or imaginary system to better understand how it works and analyze it.
    
2. **Simulation:** Experimenting on that model (usually on a computer) to predict the system's behavior or test different hypotheses.
    

Think of a **map**. A map is a _model_ of a city. It is not the city itself (it doesn't include every tree, every crack in the sidewalk, every person). It is a **simplified representation** that keeps only what you need (roads, landmarks, distances) so you can make a better decision (which route to take).

When you "list variables, eliminate negligible ones, and quantify the rest" — you are building a model. When you use that model to see what happens if you change something (e.g., "What if I leave 10 minutes later?"), you are **simulating**🎬️
