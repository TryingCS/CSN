#krr 
**Yes, there are absolutely irrational agents!**  
 _we_ design agents to do well, but an agent can still be irrational. Why? Because "acting" doesn't guarantee "good results." An agent could be poorly programmed, have faulty sensors, or use a flawed rule. For example, a simple thermostat that turns on the heat when it's already too hot is acting, but it's acting irrationally based on its goal.

The term **"Rational Agent"** is its own bullet point because it is the **gold standard** of AI design. We don't just want _any_ agent; we want one that, for every possible perception, selects the action that _maximizes its expected performance_. It is a technical benchmark
